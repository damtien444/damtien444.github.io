## About Me

<!-- **🤞 I am looking for a PhD position in generative models for the September 2025 intake or later.** -->

I am Quang-Tien Dam, a master's student at Ritsumeikan University, supervised by [Prof. Joo-Ho Lee](https://research-db.ritsumei.ac.jp/rithp/k03/resid/S000220?lang=en) and [Prof. Dinh-Tuan Tran](https://sites.google.com/view/tuantd). My master's research focuses on using generative models to generate reactions to pain stimuli signals. 

I graduated from [Danang University of Technology](https://en.dut.udn.vn/), where I was guided by [Dr. Ninh Khanh Duy](https://scv.udn.vn/nkduy) and [Dr. Le Tran Duc](https://tranducle.github.io/). I loved astronomy and was the president of the [local student astronomy club](https://thienvandanang.com/tac-gia/dam-quang-tien/), where we hosted astronomical events and created science outreach content to promote the subject. I hike in my free time and am a big nature person.

## Research Interests

My current research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and robotics. However, I'm open to exploring new horizons of machine learning applications in various areas.
